## How to use this file

1. An AI or human draft may only assert a claim that appears in the **Allowed wording** column, or a shorter subset of that wording.
2. Do not strengthen, round up, or drop a qualifier.
3. If a prospect asks for a fact that is not in this file, the correct output is: *“I don’t have an approved statement for that. I’ll confirm with the team.”*
4. Any number, registration, insurance figure, jurisdiction, or client-eligibility rule that is not listed here is **not allowed**.
5. External drafts remain **DRAFT** until a named reviewer signs off.
6. When a fact changes (volume, registration, insurance, team title), update this file first. Do not patch prompts.

**Citation rule for models:** Quote or closely paraphrase the allowed wording. Do not invent a “friendlier” version.

## 6. Custody, insurance, and security

This is the highest-risk section. Prefer the narrow wording.

| ID | Allowed wording | Source (public) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| CUST-01 | Satstreet offers clients options that may include institutional custody, self-custody, or native multi-sig solutions. | satstreet.com homepage | Do not say Satstreet is itself a qualified custodian unless Legal confirms the exact legal role. |
| CUST-02 | Satstreet uses Coinbase Custody Trust Company LLC (Coinbase Custody) as a custodian and in future may use other custodians after due diligence. | OSC public materials relating to Satstreet; consistent with firm’s public custody narrative | Confirm this is still current before repeated use. Do not say “all client assets are always at Coinbase Custody.” Public materials have also described a threshold (not less than 80% of client crypto value with third-party custodians) — **do not use the 80% figure until Compliance re-approves it as current.** |
| CUST-03 | Coinbase Custody is licensed as a limited purpose trust company with the New York Department of Financial Services. | OSC public materials relating to Satstreet | A fact about the custodian, not about Satstreet. |
| CUST-04 | In addition to benefiting from Coinbase Custody’s insurance coverage of more than US$100 million, Satstreet has increased its own financial institution bond coverage. | Satstreet press release, 2026-07-09 | **Preferred insurance sentence.** See insurance rules below. |
| CUST-05 | Satstreet has increased its business insurance coverage / financial institution bond coverage. | Same | Allowed without a dollar figure for Satstreet’s own bond, because the press release does not publish Satstreet’s limit. |

### Insurance rules (mandatory)

When CUST-04 or CUST-05 is used, the draft **must** also make these points, in substance:

- Coinbase Custody coverage is the **custodian’s** program for assets it holds, subject to that policy’s terms, limits, exclusions, and per-incident conditions.
- Satstreet’s financial institution bond is **Satstreet’s** coverage, not a wrap that makes clients whole for every loss.
- Digital assets are **not** CIPF-insured, **not** CDIC-insured, and **not** equivalent to a bank deposit.
- Insurance does **not** protect against market price decline, client error, or every form of loss.
- Do not say “your Bitcoin is insured up to $100 million.”
- Do not use the higher Coinbase parent-company figures that appear in third-party or regulatory descriptions (e.g. US$320 million) unless Satstreet’s own approved comms use that number.

## 10. Always-on disclaimers for external drafts

Append or adapt as appropriate. Do not strip these out to sound more confident.

**Short (emails, LinkedIn, one-pagers):**  
Digital assets are volatile and high risk. You can lose some or all of your capital. Satstreet does not provide investment or tax advice. Availability depends on eligibility and jurisdiction. Past volume or execution is not a guarantee of future results.

**Regulatory list:**  
Registrations described in this material identify the licences or registrations Satstreet has publicly announced. They are not a regulator’s endorsement of Satstreet, Bitcoin, or any transaction.

**Insurance:**  
Any reference to insurance describes coverage maintained by Satstreet and/or its custodian, subject to policy terms, limits, and exclusions. It is not deposit insurance and does not protect against market loss.
